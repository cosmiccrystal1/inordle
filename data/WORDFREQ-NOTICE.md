Trail weights and data/trail-quality.json are derived from wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer and Luminoso Technologies, Inc. https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq

The wordfreq code is Apache-2.0. Its combined frequency data is CC BY-SA 4.0, incorporating sources described at https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq#sources-and-supported-languages and https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq/blob/master/NOTICE.md . The derived frequency metadata and catalog weights in this project are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Changes: English frequencies were intersected with the filtered Inordle dictionary, canonicalized, and aggregated over each trail's eight most common solutions to calculate integer sampling weights.

The catalog's dictionary spelling data retains its original license, and plural mappings are generated using pluralize 8.0.0 (MIT); see DICTIONARY-LICENSE and PLURALIZE-LICENSE.
